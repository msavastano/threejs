Swirling inflow pulls fluid toward the axis while axial strain stretches the core, so vorticity grows like 1/(T*−t). Its time integral diverges, which is the Beale–Kato–Majda test for blowup. Velocity follows the Leray ansatz u = L⁻¹U(x/L). This is only an illustration: finite-energy Leray profiles are ruled out (Nečas–Růžička–Šverák, Tsai), and whether 3D Navier–Stokes solutions stay smooth is still an open Millennium Prize problem.
